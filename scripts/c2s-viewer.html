What is measured and what is drawn. Times come from the chart's own BPM / MET schedule against RESOLUTION, so horizontal position along the corridor is real. The taper is cosmetic — the recovered projection is linear in time-delta and says nothing about perspective, which sits outside the reconstruction's scope. Note speed maps to visible lookahead through the recovered materialization projection — 5388.9 / speed milliseconds — so the speed setting means roughly what it means on the cabinet. The one unproven step is that the projection's eligibility band, which is established to gate note construction, is also the visible extent. Judgement window widths are external configuration absent from the analysed binary, so the Windows overlay is driven by the slider, not by recovered values: use it to compare geometry, not to read absolute timing. Front windows are trimmed per lane against the nearest preceding note that overlaps that lane, which is the shape of the recovered rule but not its capped magnitude.

Chart regions. STP, SFL, SLP and DCM intervals are marked in the gutter off the right edge of the playfield, labelled with their scroll speed. They rewrite a pending note's scheduled position before the materialization test, and only above the 30.0F raw-delta shortcut — so they never move a near note. The bands here mark where each interval applies; the transform itself is not applied to the notes drawn, so a scrolling section will not visually speed up. SFE is recognised by the parser but has no handler case, so it is drawn dashed and dead.
